powder-coated.

## Reminders from the build guide

- Wire pots with the outer lugs to **+3V3 Analog** and **AGND** — never 9 V.
- **AGND must be tied to DGND** once, near the Seed.
- 9 V goes straight to VIN through the 1N5819 (VIN accepts +5…+17 V). No 5 V
  regulator.
- The codec is **line level** — you need the input buffer (stage 6) before a
  guitar sounds right.
